# Question

Do small real-estate offices in Turkey lose deals because customer requests
are tracked by hand, and would they pay for a tool that remembers every request
and matches it to new listings?

## Hypotheses
1. Offices lose customers because a matching listing appears weeks later and nobody remembers who asked.
2. Requests live in notebooks, WhatsApp chats and memory, not in software.
3. An office would pay a monthly fee for this if setup takes under an hour.

## Sources
App store reviews of real-estate CRM apps · public forums and Reddit ·
YouTube interviews with agents · complaint sites · price pages of existing tools ·
long-running paid ads.

## Right voice
Office owners and agents who pay for tools. Not home buyers.
